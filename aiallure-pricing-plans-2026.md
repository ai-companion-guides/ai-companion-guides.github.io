# AiAllure Pricing & Plans (2026)

*Updated November 2026 – SFW research only.*

AiAllure has one big pricing difference from other AI companion apps: **paid plans don't use tokens for core pictures and video.** So the question isn't "how many credits do I need", it's "which plan fits how I use it".

---

## 💶 All plans

| Plan | Price | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter yearly** | **$118.80/year = $9.90/month** | Most people: the full experience at half the monthly price |
| Starter monthly | $19.90/month | Trying it for a month first |
| Pro | $39.90/month | Heavy users who want more capacity |
| Power | $59.90/month | The most capacity |
| Lifetime | One-time, from $199 | Long-term users who prefer to pay once |

## 🧮 How it compares

On token-based apps, a busy month of pictures and video can cost the subscription price again in top-ups. On AiAllure the core use is included, so **$9.90 a month on the yearly plan is the real price**, not a starting price.

Compared with OurDream ($9.99/month yearly, about 10 standard clips included) and Candy AI ($3.99/month yearly, media on tokens), AiAllure costs the same or a little more but stays flat however much you generate.
