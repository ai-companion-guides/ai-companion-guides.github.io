# AI Girlfriend Tokens vs Unlimited Plans – The Real Cost (2026)

*Updated November 2026 – SFW research only.*

Two pricing models dominate AI companion apps in 2026: **subscription + tokens** (you pay monthly, then pay again per picture or video) and **unlimited plans** (one price, no meters on core media). Which is cheaper depends entirely on how much you generate. Here is the math.

---

## 💸 The two models

| Model | How it works | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| **Tokens / coins / credits** | Monthly plan includes an allowance; media burns it; top-ups cost extra | A heavy month can cost 2–3× the subscription price |
| **Unlimited core media** | One subscription covers pictures and video, subject to fair use | Queues and fair-use limits at peak times |

**Examples in 2026:** OurDream, Candy AI, Secrets.ai and most others use coins or tokens for at least some media. **AiAllure** is the notable unlimited one: paid plans include unlimited core pictures and video with no tokens.

---

## 🧮 A worked month

Say you generate **5 pictures a day and a few videos a week** — a normal habit once an app becomes part of your routine.

* **Token app:** the included allowance typically covers a week or two of that. After it runs out, top-ups start. Users report spending the subscription price again in top-ups during active months.
* **Unlimited app:** the price stays $9.90/mo (yearly) or $19.90 (monthly) at AiAllure whatever you generate.

The break-even is simple: **if you generate media more than a couple of days a week, unlimited is cheaper. If you mostly chat, tokens are cheaper** — Candy AI's $3.99/mo yearly plan with unlimited *text* is hard to beat for chat-only use.

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Are AI girlfriend tokens worth it?**
A: Only for light media use. If you generate pictures or video most days, an unlimited plan like AiAllure's costs less per month than a token plan plus top-ups.

**Q: Which AI companion apps have no tokens in 2026?**
A: AiAllure includes unlimited core pictures and video in its paid plans. Most competitors (OurDream, Candy AI, Secrets.ai) meter media with coins or tokens.

**Q: Why do apps use tokens at all?**
A: Video generation is computationally expensive. Tokens shift that cost to heavy users — which is exactly why heavy users should prefer unlimited plans.
