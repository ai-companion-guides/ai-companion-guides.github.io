# Image to Video in AI Companion Apps (2026)

*Updated November 2026 – SFW research only.*

Most AI companion apps now let you take a picture of your companion and turn it into a short moving clip. The workflow is the same everywhere: **create the companion → generate a picture you like → animate it**. What differs is clip length, motion quality and price per clip.

---

## 🧭 How it works, step by step

1. **Create the companion** on a free account: look, style, personality.
2. **Generate pictures** until you have one you like. Better source pictures give better video.
3. **Animate it.** Pick the picture, choose a motion or prompt, and the app renders a short clip, usually 5–10 seconds, longer on some plans.
